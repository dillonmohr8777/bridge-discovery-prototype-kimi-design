# Bridge Phase 2 Product Context

## Product truth

Bridge is a verified cannabis-industry network that connects community participation, trusted business identity, nationwide discovery, and permissioned introductions. The Phase 2 prototype is a product-definition artifact. It demonstrates intended information architecture and interaction rules; it does not claim production data, completed authentication, legal approval, or live marketplace operations.

Core positioning: **One industry. One network. One Bridge.**

## Primary audiences

- Adults aged 21 and older using public or member-safe content.
- Industry members who participate in Community News and eligible signals.
- EIN-verified businesses with protected company and relationship data.
- Retailers and dispensaries that maintain menus, locations, brand relationships, and operational contacts.
- Brands, vendors, laboratories, logistics providers, banks, construction firms, and other industry services.
- Authorized sales, accounting, and administrative staff.

## Phase 2 product spaces

| Space | Route | Job to be done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Understand Bridge and enter the right product space. |
| Community News | `/community/` | Scan visual stories, events, promotions, business updates, and private signals. |
| Create | `/studio/` | Build compliant content, upload an asset, and target eligible audiences. |
| My Profile | `/business/` | Manage public identity, verified business details, contacts, and access. |
| Explore | `/signal/` | Find and favorite nationwide businesses, products, brands, services, and market opportunities. |

## Phase 2 success criteria

1. A first-time reviewer can name the five spaces and move between them without translation.
2. Public, member, and verified-business information are visually and behaviorally distinct.
3. A creator can upload PNG, JPEG, WebP, or PDF content and choose more than one eligible audience.
4. Protected business material cannot be targeted to a public audience.
5. A member can search and filter illustrative nationwide records, favorite them, and request permissioned introductions.
6. A business owner sees current sales and accounting contacts and understands the recurring confirmation requirement.
7. The feed comparison lets stakeholders decide between a staggered news grid and a classic vertical feed using the same content.
8. Responsive layouts work without horizontal overflow at desktop and 390-pixel mobile widths.

## Product principles

- Trust before reach: identity and eligibility gates are part of the experience, not back-office detail.
- Useful signal over popularity theater: show context, sample size, timing, and limitations rather than public vanity counts.
- Permission before introduction: protected contact details are not exchanged automatically.
- Visual discovery with operational depth: imagery and movement invite exploration, while filters and profiles support real work.
- One system, different views: roles change visibility and actions without creating disconnected products.

## Explicit non-goals for this prototype

- Final subscription pricing or packaging.
- Production authentication, EIN verification, file storage, menu/order APIs, maps, social, or CRM integrations.
- A final vendor-to-vendor visibility rule without stakeholder and legal review.
- HR record management, performance tracking, or employee-surveillance features. Tori described this as a future concept that requires separate legal, privacy, and product discovery.
- Claims that illustrative companies, signals, counts, or credentials are live.
