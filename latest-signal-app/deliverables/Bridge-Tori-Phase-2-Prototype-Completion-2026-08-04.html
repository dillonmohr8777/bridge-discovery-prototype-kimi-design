Bridge Software | Dillon-owned next actions08
Beyond the literal feedback

Additional Phase 2 work the product now entails.

Tori's review defines the experience. A responsible production path also needs the following foundations, even where the transcript did not specify implementation detail.

Identity, privacy, and authorization

  • Verified server claims and delegated staff permissions.
  • Field-level projection rules and audit events.
  • Privacy notice, consent, retention, and data-access decisions.
  • Legal review of advertising, state coverage, verification, and introductions.

Data and integration quality

  • Authoritative legal-state and business sources with freshness rules.
  • Upload storage, malware scanning, transformation, and PDF preview.
  • Menu/order, maps, social, Leafly, and email provider choices.
  • Explicit behavior for missing, stale, conflicting, or partial fields.

Operations and safety

  • Verification queue, renewal, appeal, and escalation.
  • Content moderation, reports, takedowns, and protected-content incidents.
  • Contact-reminder ownership and introduction approval workflow.
  • Support playbook, rollback path, and launch incident ownership.

Evidence and release quality

  • Automated acceptance tests for every selected journey and role.
  • Keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, reduced-motion, zoom, and mobile regression.
  • Analytics definitions that separate delivery, activity, lead quality, and verified outcomes.
  • Pilot success criteria and network-density thresholds before public expansion.
